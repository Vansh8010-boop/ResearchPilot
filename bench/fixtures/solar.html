Rooftop Solar Panel Test: Results

We tested 12 monocrystalline rooftop panels over six months. This page is a test fixture for the ResearchPilot benchmark.

Results

The average efficiency of the panels tested was 21.4 percent. The best panel reached 23.1 percent and the weakest 18.9 percent. Output fell by an average of 0.4 percent per degree Celsius above 25 degrees.

Method
