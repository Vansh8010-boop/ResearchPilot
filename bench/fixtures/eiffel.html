The Eiffel Tower in Numbers

The Eiffel Tower was built for the 1889 World's Fair in Paris and was the tallest man-made structure in the world until 1930.

Height

Including its antennas, the tower is 330 metres tall. Without antennas the structure measures 300 metres. The top public platform is at 276 metres.

Construction

The tower is made of about 18,000 wrought-iron parts held together by 2.5 million rivets. It took 2 years, 2 months and 5 days to build, and visitors can climb 674 steps to the second floor.
