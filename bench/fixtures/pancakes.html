Classic Pancakes

Makes 8 pancakes. Ready in 25 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 200 g plain flour
  • 300 ml milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • A pinch of salt
  • Butter, for the pan

Method

Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt. Beat the eggs into the milk, then whisk the wet ingredients into the dry ones until smooth. Rest the batter for 10 minutes. Cook ladlefuls in a buttered pan for about 1 minute on each side.
